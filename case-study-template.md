# Case Study Template

Schema and story beats for portfolio case studies. The synthesis agent uses this alongside `voice.md` when drafting from project journals.

---

## Structure

### 1. Hook (1–2 sentences)
What was the problem and why did it matter? Lead with impact or stakes, not background.

### 2. My role
What were you responsible for? What wasn't your responsibility?

### 3. The challenge
Context on the constraints, complexity, or ambiguity that made this hard.

### 4. What I did
The work. Decisions made, approaches taken. This is where the journal content lands.
- Be specific about *what you decided* and *why*, not just what you built
- Highlight pivots: what changed mid-project and why

### 5. Key numbers
<!-- Big numbers get their own space — don't bury them in prose -->
Results that are measurable. Even rough numbers are better than none.

### 6. What I'd do differently
Honest reflection. Shows self-awareness and growth.

### 7. Takeaway (1–3 sentences)
What's the one thing someone should remember from this project?

---

## Structural preferences

- Lead with the problem, not the org chart
- Big numbers get their own line — don't bury them in a sentence
- 3 punchy recommendations, not a wall of bullets
- Avoid "I was responsible for" — prefer active verbs: "I designed", "I led", "I built"
- No jargon without explanation
- Honest about what went wrong — this is a strength, not a weakness

## Sections that are optional

- **Testimonial** — include if you have a strong quote
- **Process visuals** — wireframes, diagrams, journey maps if relevant
- **Team context** — only if the team dynamic is part of the story

## What to leave out

- Org hierarchy and reporting lines (unless directly relevant)
- Tool names for their own sake ("I used Figma") — focus on decisions, not tools
- Apologies for scope ("this was a small project but...")
