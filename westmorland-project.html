Westmorland & Furness Council

Digital Ambition for Family Help

Worked alongside a Lead Service Designer and technical Consultant to help the council define an approach to expanding the digital element of their Family Hub offering, which consists of a blend of physical and digital provision.

The project culminated in the collaborative production of a high-level roadmap and accompanying reports, documenting recommendations, options and dependencies, while balancing the needs and strategies of several internal teams and stakeholders.

The digital ambition is to leverage technology to enhance the accessibility, efficiency, and effectiveness of support.

Case Study

Context

Westmorland & Furness Council sought to enhance their Family Hub offering by integrating digital solutions to complement their physical services. The goal was to create a seamless experience for families accessing support.

Family Hubs are services that provide support and advice for families and young people.

A big component of that provision is delivered in person, from public spaces like libraies and community centres.

Problem Space

The council faced challenges in ensuring equitable access to services, managing resources efficiently, and aligning digital initiatives with the needs of diverse stakeholders.

Approach

  • Stakeholder Interviews

    Conducted in-depth interviews with key council staff and partners to understand current challenges, needs, and aspirations for digital transformation.

  • Running Workshops

    Facilitated collaborative workshops to map existing services, identify pain points, and generate ideas for digital improvements.

  • User Research

    _

    "The only time I've been on the council website was to get my Disability Bus Pass... it took an effort to find the right page, I went to the Government site, then the council, then just Googled a load of stuff"

    — Local user

  • Analysis

    User Insights

    • Fragmented content was preventing users from finding information.

    • Parents generally gravitate towards in-person provision.

    • Rural surroundings and poverty impacted users' ability to access services.

    Internal Insights

    • Staff were spending significant time and effort on basic support and admin work.

    • Peer to peer support groups have proven difficult to manage.

    • Summat else.

  • Remote Co-creation Workshop

    Hosted an online co-creation session with stakeholders to collaboratively design potential digital service journeys and features.

  • Roadmapping

    Developed a high-level roadmap outlining phased implementation, dependencies, and priorities for digital initiatives.

What We Delivered

  • A high-level roadmap outlining key milestones and dependencies.
  • Detailed reports documenting recommendations and options.
  • Guidance on aligning digital strategies with organizational goals.

Outcomes

The project resulted in a clear vision for the digital expansion of the Family Hub, enabling the council to move forward with confidence. Stakeholders were aligned on priorities, and the groundwork was laid for future implementation.
