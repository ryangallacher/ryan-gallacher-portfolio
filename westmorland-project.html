Westmorland & Furness Council

Digital Ambition for Family Help

SL: How Westmorland and Furness Council's Digital and Family Help Teams found clarity and reduced operational pressure through collaborative service design.

SL: The service offers practical and emotional support to families.

SL: An existing website offered some relevant services to families, but there was no overall integration between the physical and digital offerings.

SL: Leveraging technology to enhance service provision

SL: Westmorland and Furness Council had recently disaggregated from Cumbria County Council, creating significant operational pressures. The Family Help Team, responsible for crucial community support services, needed assistance from the Digital Innovation Team as part of their transformation strategy.

Without a clear roadmap, the Family Help Team’s requests of the Digital Team risked being scattergun and uncoordinated, putting pressure on the Digital Team and potentially lowering the quality of the end result.

SL: We began by thoroughly examining the operational successes and challenges of the Barrow family hub. Leveraging a structured service design methodology, we facilitated extensive workshops and roundtable discussions involving both the family and digital teams. Crucially, they included specialist content editors and designers to ensure proposed digital solutions were intuitive, accessible, and flexible. Through collaborative mapping and open dialogue, stakeholders gained a clear view of their current service ecosystem. This helped to identify areas for prioritisation and collaboration between the Digital Team and the Family Help Team.

SL: Scott Logic designed a blueprint for virtual family hubs that provided consistency while allowing flexibility to address specific local needs.

Worked alongside a Lead Service Designer and technical Consultant to help the council define an approach to expanding the digital element of their Family Hub offering, which consists of a blend of physical and digital provision.

The project culminated in the collaborative production of a high-level roadmap and accompanying reports, documenting recommendations, options and dependencies, while balancing the needs and strategies of several internal teams and stakeholders.

The digital ambition is to leverage technology to enhance the accessibility, efficiency, and effectiveness of support.

Scott Logic's collaborative approach helped us unify our thinking and clearly see the path forward. They brought everyone to the table and gave us the clarity we needed.

— Helen Blake, Assistant Director of Customer and Digital, Westmorland and Furness Council

Case Study

Context

Westmorland & Furness Council sought to enhance their Family Hub offering by integrating digital solutions to complement their physical services. The goal was to create a seamless experience for families accessing support.

Family Hubs are services that provide support and advice for families and young people.

A big component of that provision is delivered in person, from public spaces like libraies and community centres.

Problem Space

The council faced challenges in ensuring equitable access to services, managing resources efficiently, and aligning digital initiatives with the needs of diverse stakeholders.

Approach

  • Stakeholder Interviews

    Conducted in-depth interviews with key council staff and partners to understand current challenges, needs, and aspirations for digital transformation.

  • Running Workshops

    Facilitated collaborative workshops to map existing services, identify pain points, and generate ideas for digital improvements.

  • User Research

    _

    "The only time I've been on the council website was to get my Disability Bus Pass... it took an effort to find the right page, I went to the Government site, then the council, then just Googled a load of stuff"

    — Local user

  • Analysis

    User Insights

    • Fragmented content was preventing users from finding information.

    • Parents generally gravitate towards in-person provision.

    • Rural surroundings and poverty impacted users' ability to access services.

    Internal Insights

    • Staff were spending significant time and effort on basic support and admin work.

    • Peer to peer support groups have proven difficult to manage.

    • Summat else.

  • Remote Co-creation Workshop

    Hosted an online co-creation session with stakeholders to collaboratively design potential digital service journeys and features.

  • Roadmapping

    Developed a high-level roadmap outlining phased implementation, dependencies, and priorities for digital initiatives.

What We Delivered

  • A high-level roadmap outlining key milestones and dependencies.
  • Detailed reports documenting recommendations and options.
  • Guidance on aligning digital strategies with organizational goals.

Outcomes

SL: With Scott Logic’s collaborative, user-focused approach, Westmorland and Furness Council has a plan to transform its service delivery. They now enjoy streamlined operations, clearer communications, and a sustainable roadmap for future digital and physical expansion, ready to adapt swiftly to future challenges.

SL: delivered clarity, alignment, and practical outcomes that promise to transform the operational effectiveness of both public-facing services, and the way the Family Help and Digital Teams work together. The Family Help Team achieved clearer workflows and a cohesive operational focus, dramatically reducing their need for ad-hoc digital support. This allowed the Digital Team to shift from reactive support roles into strategic delivery. Additionally, the flexible and scalable virtual hub model provided the council with a clear pathway to expanding their family service provision while they made longer-term plans to build more physical hubs. Now confident in the approach, Westmorland and Furness Council can efficiently replicate services across communities by working collaboratively and smartly using internal resources.

The project resulted in a clear vision for the digital expansion of the Family Hub, enabling the council to move forward with confidence. Stakeholders were aligned on priorities, and the groundwork was laid for future implementation.
