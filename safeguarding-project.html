NDA

Safeguarding Project

Case Study

Context

Worked with a cross-functional team to define the vision and strategic roadmap for an improved safeguarding system, used by casework professionals to protect vulnerable individuals. Played a hands-on role in defining and validating project goals, requirements and risks through several workshops and 15 in-person User Research sessions. Uncovered key insights that informed priortisation, ideation and final recommendations - these included workarounds, dupliaction of effort, opportunities for automation and wider issues around policy and ways of working. Worked with Design, BA and technical colleagues to define high level 'TO-BE' service maps and diagrams that covered the core elements of user workflows for in-scope case types. collaboratively created decision trees, relationsip diagrams, user flows and wireframes to make thinking tangible and (testable). Refined this thinking into Figma mockups using Design System components. Presented these designs, rationale and areas for future exploration to key stakeholders in playback sessions.

Problem Space

Critical safeguarding service is (run on) an outdated, third-party system, hosting location due to close. Dual systems causing duplication of effort, risk and manual workarounds were another key driver for this piece of work. Service vision: Create a modern, unified safeguarding system that enables efficient, data-driven decision making and protects vulnerable individuals. Aligned with the organisation's broader strategic aims: (Digital First), efficient operations and proactive safeguarding.

Approach

  • Stakeholder Interviews

    Conducted in-depth interviews with key council staff and partners to understand current challenges, needs, and aspirations for digital transformation.

  • Running Workshops

    Facilitated collaborative workshops to map existing services, identify pain points, and generate ideas for digital improvements.

  • User Research

    _

    "The only time I've been on the council website was to get my Disability Bus Pass... it took an effort to find the right page, I went to the Government site, then the council, then just Googled a load of stuff"

    — Local user

  • Analysis

    User Insights

    • Fragmented content was preventing users from finding information.

    • Parents generally gravitate towards in-person provision.

    • Rural surroundings and poverty impacted users' ability to access services.

    Internal Insights

    • Staff were spending significant time and effort on basic support and admin work.

    • Peer to peer support groups have proven difficult to manage.

    • Summat else.

  • Remote Co-creation Workshop

    Hosted an online co-creation session with stakeholders to collaboratively design potential digital service journeys and features.

  • Roadmapping

    Developed a high-level roadmap outlining phased implementation, dependencies, and priorities for digital initiatives.

What We Delivered

  • A high-level roadmap outlining key milestones and dependencies.
  • Detailed reports documenting recommendations and options.
  • Guidance on aligning digital strategies with organizational goals.

Outcomes

The project resulted in a clear vision for the digital expansion of the Family Hub, enabling the council to move forward with confidence. Stakeholders were aligned on priorities, and the groundwork was laid for future implementation.
